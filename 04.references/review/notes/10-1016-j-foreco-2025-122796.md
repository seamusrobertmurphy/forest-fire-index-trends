Question Q3, how fire danger climatology and Fire Weather Index thresholds have been set against observed fires in British Columbia, and what a Forest Ecology and Management paper on fire danger looks like as a structural exemplar.

Establishes Perrakis, Brown, Morrison, Horrelt and Taylor built a fire danger climatology for the Greater Victoria water supply area from 1996 to 2022, set the threshold for locally significant fires at a Fire Weather Index of 32, about the 93rd percentile, and for landscape fires near 50, about the 99.6th percentile, and reported higher Fire Weather Index quantiles in 2010 to 2022 than in 1996 to 2009.
