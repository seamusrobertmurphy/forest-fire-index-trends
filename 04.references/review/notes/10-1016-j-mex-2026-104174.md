Establishes
Wilkinson, Verkaik, Thompson, Waddington, Arango Ruda and Wotton published the daily Peat Moisture Code and named it the basis of the hourly code to be added to FWI2025, citing the 2025 update report GLC-X-42 as their reference 3, but the paper evaluates neither FWI2025 against the 1987 System nor hourly against daily input. The full text (7,429 words, Europe PMC record PMC13634467) was searched; it names FWI2025 three times and compares no index series.
