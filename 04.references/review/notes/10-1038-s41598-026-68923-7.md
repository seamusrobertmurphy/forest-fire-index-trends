Establishes Akdagli, Scientific Reports, article in press (2026), no volume or pages yet, computed the six components of the Canadian Fire Weather Index System at each of 41 ERA5 cells intersecting Mersin Province, Türkiye, from hourly ERA5 fields sampled at 10:00 UTC, with relative humidity from the dew point, 24 hour precipitation ending at that hour, and a fixed start on 1 June each year at FFMC 85, DMC 6 and DC 15 with no overwintering, for the June to October seasons of 2003 to 2025, then aggregated the cell values by intersection area. The target was whether the area weighted mean index on the next day reached 21.3, the lower bound of the EFFIS high class, which held on 63.6 per cent of 3,519 days. A 16 predictor L2 regularised logistic model, with lagged and rolling index values, current weather, antecedent rain, the fraction of the province above threshold and sine and cosine of season day, was compared with persistence, a climatology, a 62 predictor logistic model and three tree ensembles on six expanding whole season folds over 2003 to 2023, scored by the Brier score first, and then on a held-out 2024 to 2025 block, where it reached a ROC AUC of 0.946, an average precision of 0.971, a Brier score of 0.086 and a calibration slope of 0.962. Persistence alone reached a ROC AUC of 0.892 in development, and the ensembles gave no reproducible gain. A sensitivity run at 09:00 UTC correlated with the 10:00 UTC series at 0.992 yet averaged 2.87 units lower, and the binary high class agreed on only 91.6 per cent of days. Annual mean FWI ran from 19.50 in 2014 to 29.49 in 2013 with no monotonic progression, but no trend was estimated, no fire occurrence was modelled and hourly input was never set against a once-daily alternative; FWI2025 was not named.
