Question. Q1.

Establishes. Amiro, Logan, Wotton, Flannigan, Todd, Stocks and Martell (International Journal of Wildland Fire 13, 391 to 400, 2004) computed the highest noon Fire Weather Index System values in the first 21 days of 9333 fires over 2 square kilometres in the boreal and taiga ecozones for 1959 to 1999 and found no trend in most components over the 41 years because of large inter annual variability. The abstract names no estimator, no autocorrelation treatment and no correction.

Quotations

"Canadian Fire Weather Index (FWI) System components and head fire intensities were calculated for fires greater than 2 km2 in size for the boreal and taiga ecozones of Canada from 1959 to 1999." (Abstract)

"Most FWI System parameters did not show trends over the 41-year period because of large inter-annual variability." (Abstract)

"A changing climate is expected to create future weather conditions more conducive to fire throughout much of Canada but clear changes have not yet occurred." (Abstract)
