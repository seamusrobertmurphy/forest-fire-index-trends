Question Q3, the precedent for a negative binomial model of daily fire counts against fuel moisture with an independent year held out.

Establishes Plucinski, McCaw, Gould and Wotton modelled daily counts of human caused bushfires in ten management areas of south west Western Australia by negative binomial regression on fuel moisture, recent fire counts, work day and rainfall, fitted on three years and evaluated on an independent year. No open copy exists and the CSIRO hosts blocked retrieval on 2026-10-13, so only the Crossref abstract was read, as in the manuscript already. International Journal of Wildland Fire 23(4), 520 to 531.

Quotations

"Models were developed using negative binomial regression with a dataset covering 3 years and evaluated using data from an independent year." (abstract)

"A common model form that included variables relating to fuel moisture content, the number of recent human-caused bushfires, work day (binary classification separating weekends and public holidays from other days) and rainfall was applied to all areas." (abstract)

"The model had reasonable fit statistics across all management areas, but showed enough day-to-day prediction variability to be of practical use only in the more densely populated management areas, which were dominated by deliberate ignitions." (abstract)
