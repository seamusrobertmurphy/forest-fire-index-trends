Hanes, Wotton, Bourgeau-Chavez, Woolford, Bélair, Martell and Flannigan compared the daily Drought Code with hourly soil moisture probes at seven plots in Alberta and Ontario and with a land surface model, and found the Drought Code tracked the 10 to 18 cm organic layer well; the paper predates the 2025 update and names neither it nor any hourly version of the System, and it compares no hourly against daily index.
