Establishes
Perello and co-authors tested the hourly RISICO indices against daily FWI, ISI, FFMC and FFDI over 16 years of Italian fires, aggregating the hourly indices to a daily value before comparison, and reported area under the curve of 0.86 for RISICO rate of spread against 0.82 for FWI overall, with the hourly index classifying 2.32 per cent of the territory as High or Extreme in summer against 6.01 per cent for FWI while catching a similar share of fire pixels.
