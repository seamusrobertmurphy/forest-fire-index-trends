Question. Q1.

Establishes. Németh, Hübnerová and Zempléni (Communications in Statistics, Simulation and Computation, 2020) showed by bootstrap simulation that the likelihood ratio test for a trend in a generalised extreme value model has large decision errors at realistic sample sizes, proposed a simulation based correction and a return level method, and reassessed trends in monthly maxima of the Fire Weather Index at selected British Columbia stations. The abstract reports no slope, station count, period or correction.
