Question Q3, how fire occurrence and season length have been related to fire weather over a long record in northern Canada, and which Fire Weather Index threshold separated extreme years.

Establishes Gaboriau and co-authors described fire occurrence, size, cause and season length in the Tłı̨chǫ territory from 1965 to 2019 against interpolated seasonal fire weather, and found the nine extreme years exceeded a Drought Code of 514, an Initial Spread Index of 7 and a Fire Weather Index of 19, the same value as the spread day threshold the manuscript uses. Taylor and Francis and the HAL mirror both blocked retrieval on 2026-10-13, so only the OpenAlex abstract was read and the statistical tests could not be quoted. Ecoscience 29(3), 249 to 265.
