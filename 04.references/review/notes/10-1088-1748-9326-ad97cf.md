Question Q3, how Fire Weather Index thresholds for fire danger classes relate to fire occurrence and whether they are set per region.

Establishes Kudláčková and co-authors reviewed 750 papers and generalised Fire Weather Index danger classes across 18 global environmental zones, compared the values with fire occurrence, and found that high danger sits near a Fire Weather Index of 50 in drier and warmer climates while extreme danger sits near 25 in cooler and moister ones, so classes are set by regional climate rather than one global scale.
