Establishes
Luo, Wang, Castellanos-Acuña and Flannigan trained a random forest on hourly GOES-R active fire detections for 8,993 North American fires of 2017 to 2023 with hourly ERA5 weather and hourly and daily CFWIS components as predictors, then reconstructed potential burning hours from 1975 to 2024 and found them up 36 per cent, with Mann-Kendall significant increases over 52 per cent of burnable area and 4 to 5 potential burning hours per year gained across 66 to 89 per cent of the burnable area of British Columbia, Alberta and Yukon. The hourly inputs are the whole design and no daily-input series is set against them, so the paper shows a trend in an hourly quantity rather than a trend difference between inputs. The full text (9,705 words, Europe PMC record PMC13089344) was searched and holds no mention of FWI2025, the 2025 update, NG-CFFDRS or CFFDRS2025.
