Question Q3, because it describes the atmospheric conditions under which lightning ignites fires in the interior Pacific Northwest next to the Okanagan, dry and unstable lower air with little rain reaching the ground, and records that fire start dates lag ignition, both of which bear on how daily ignition counts relate to fire weather, though it fitted no count model.

Establishes Rorig and Ferguson, in Journal of Applied Meteorology 38(11), 1565 to 1575, separated wet from dry thunderstorm days at Spokane, Washington, over May to September of 1948 to 1977 by a rainfall threshold of 2.54 mm, which gave 173 dry and 133 wet days, reduced to 135 and 101 with a 0000 UTC sounding, and built a maximum likelihood discriminant rule from the two sounding variables that separated the groups, the 85 kPa dewpoint depression and the 85 to 50 kPa temperature difference. The rule classified 73 per cent of the Spokane days correctly on the data it was built from, and 58 to 74 per cent at Yakima, Walla Walla, Pendleton, Baker and Idaho Falls over the same period, while the K index, total totals and Haines index did not separate the groups. Composite 50 kPa height maps showed a trough over the Pacific coast on both kinds of day, with the trough axis farther west and heights higher on dry days, and no significant difference in sea level pressure. With Bureau of Land Management lightning detection data for 1986 to 1996, a lightning day being one with a strike within 10 km of a station, the original rule classified 57 to 73 per cent of days correctly and a rule refitted on 1986 to 1996 soundings 64 to 83 per cent, on samples of 7 to 36 wet and 33 to 207 dry days. Two days in July 1989 in the Columbia River Basin, one classed wet and one dry, showed many more fire starts on the dry day, but the paper noted that fire start dates record detection rather than ignition, which may follow days later, so no quantitative link between strikes and starts was made. No fire danger index, count distribution, seasonal term, cross-validation beyond the independent stations, or test of change in the relationship over time was used.
