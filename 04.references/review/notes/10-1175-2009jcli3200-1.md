Question. Q2, with the Canadian wind trend bearing on Q1 as a Fire Weather Index input.

Establishes. Wan, Wang and Swail (Journal of Climate 23, 1209 to 1225, 2010) homogenised monthly mean wind speed at 117 Canadian stations for 1953 to 2006, adjusting hourly speeds to 10 m by a logarithmic profile and testing against geostrophic wind reference series with the penalised maximal t and F tests, found anemometer height change at 114 of 117 stations and relocation as the main causes of shifts, and estimated linear trends with lag one autocorrelation fitted in tandem, which showed significant decreases through western and southern Canada in all seasons; raw and homogenised trends were often of opposite sign in the western mountains, and one shift of 3.03 km per hour turned a homogenised trend of 0.0246 km per hour per year into a raw trend of minus 0.0768.
