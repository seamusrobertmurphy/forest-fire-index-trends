Establishes
Fox-Hughes computed the McArthur Mark V forest fire danger index from half-hourly airport observations at three Tasmanian stations and found that 3-hourly and 1500 LT sampling miss many threshold exceedances, with only half of the diurnal peaks above the warning level at Hobart falling at 1500 LT, so sampling frequency changes the count of threshold days.
