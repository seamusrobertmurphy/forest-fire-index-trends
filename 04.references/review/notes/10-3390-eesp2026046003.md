Establishes
Campos, Couto, Guiomar and Salgado computed an hourly FWI from 2.5 km and 500 m Meso-NH simulations of two Portuguese fire periods in 2017 and 2018 and compared it with the daily Copernicus product at 1200 UTC, finding a diurnal cycle and a negative bias of the model in both cases but reporting no numbers in the text.
