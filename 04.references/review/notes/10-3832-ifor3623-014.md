Question Q2, because it compared the same daily ignition model driven by once-daily observer stations against automatic stations logging 48 to 144 times a day and found the once-daily source predicted better, which it put down to station density; and Q3, as a comparator for daily ignition modelling by logistic regression with month-specific models in place of a seasonal term and a held-out year for validation, outside Canada and without the Fire Weather Index.

Establishes Vilchis-Francés, Díaz-Delgado, Becerril Piña, Mastachi Loza, Gómez-Albores and Bâ, iForest 14, 437 to 446, built 252 logistic regression models of daily fire ignition at 1 km pixels over 53,962 square kilometres of the Mexican highlands, one model for each of six fire season months, January to June, each of seven vegetation types and each of six combinations of weather source and drought index, trained on 2,320 fires recorded by the State of Mexico forest protection agency over 2006 to 2015 and validated on 2015 alone. The dryness predictors were the effective drought index and the standardised precipitation evapotranspiration index, computed daily from precipitation and maximum and minimum temperature at 748 conventional stations read once a day by an observer over 2006 to 2014 and at 124 automatic stations over 2012 to 2014; distance to roads, slope, aspect and a monthly mean maximum wind completed the predictors. Skill was scored by the relative operating characteristic and by model precision efficiency from the confusion matrix at the median training probability. The automatic station models with the standardised index gave the highest median ROC of 0.757 against 0.734 for the conventional station equivalent, yet more conventional station monthly models cleared the 0.700 threshold, nine against seven, and the authors recommended the denser once-daily network. The Fire Weather Index was not used, counts were not modelled, no autocorrelation or multiple testing treatment was reported, and no change over time in the danger to ignition relation was tested.
