Establishes
Quilcaille, Batibeniz, Ribeiro, Padrón and Seneviratne computed the FWI for 1,321 CMIP6 runs from daily maximum temperature and daily minimum or daily mean relative humidity in place of noon values, with an algorithm adjusted for overwintering, and found that daily mean relative humidity lowers the fire season FWI by about 33 per cent above 20° N against daily minimum relative humidity while the reduction is the same in 1994 to 2014 and 2081 to 2100, so the input choice moves the level but, by their reading, not the relative change. The full text (25 pages, 13,223 words) was searched and holds no mention of FWI2025, the 2025 update, NG-CFFDRS or CFFDRS2025; the only "next generation" is the name of a data archive.
