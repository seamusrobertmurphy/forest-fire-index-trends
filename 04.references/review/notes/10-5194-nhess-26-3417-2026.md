Question
Q2 and Q4.

Establishes
Mirones and co-authors computed a reference FWI from ERA5-Land at 12 UTC over the Iberian Peninsula and a "Proxy" FWI from daily aggregates, and showed that the proxy biases the frequency of days above the 95th percentile in both directions by region and overestimates extreme spell length by up to 4 days, with a mean absolute error of 8.77 on the 95th percentile against 8.19 for the best deep learning emulator over the 2018 to 2021 test period. Trends were not compared. The full text (26 pages, 16,312 words) was searched and holds no mention of FWI2025, the 2025 update, NG-CFFDRS or CFFDRS2025.

"In these instances, a “Proxy” of actual FWI is often used, applying the same FWI formulation to daily aggregated values (mean, max, or min), despite known limitations in capturing extremes and temporal dynamics." (p. 3417)

"However, the resulting daily mean FWI proxies cannot be reliably transformed to match their instantaneous counterparts, leading to inconsistent future projections (Herrera et al., 2013)." (p. 3418)

"Regarding FWI95 MAE, the U-Net model improves upon the Proxy FWI results (8.19 vs. 8.77), offering a smoother MAE distribution across the region and particularly low MAE values in some continental areas in Spain, where the Proxy FWI exhibits large errors." (p. 3425)
