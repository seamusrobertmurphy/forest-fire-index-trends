Question
Q4 and Q2.

Establishes
Oliveira, Maia and Praça built a pipeline that runs WRF at 9 km over the Iberian Peninsula and computes the six FWI System components hourly in Python, validated the weather fields against ERA5-Land for six days of 2025 and compared the FFMC map at 12:00 UTC with the official Portuguese map, but reported no quantitative comparison of hourly against daily index values and no trend. The full text (8 pages, 4,627 words, printed pages 434 to 441) was searched and holds no mention of FWI2025, the 2025 update, NG-CFFDRS or CFFDRS2025; the hourly calculation is presented as the authors' own extension of the once-daily system of Van Wagner (1987).

Quotations
"A case study also demonstrates the automated calculation of an empirical fire index and its components, including the Fine Fuel Moisture Code (FFMC) and Buildup Index (BUI), at hourly resolution using open-source Python tools." (p. 434)
