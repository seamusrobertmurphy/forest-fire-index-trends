Subject. The text listed the publications of Canada's federal forest fire research staff, including the danger tables and the early reports on the Fire Weather Index.

Establishes. Ramsey and Bruce listed federal fire research publications of 1909 to 1974 in 18 sections lettered A to R. Section K listed regional danger tables of 1956 to 1968, then the Canadian fire weather index of 1969, provisional, and of 1970. Entries of 1968 and 1969 recorded drought index comparisons, a modular national design, four Petawawa reports on danger development and a Kananaskis meeting on the Index. The list included a 1970 computer program for the Index by Simard and Van Wagner's 1974 report on its structure. British Columbia entries included the Coast and Cariboo tables, a diurnal adjustment table for the Fine Fuel Moisture Code and Turner's 1970 calibration of the Index. The text gave titles only, with no abstracts or findings.
